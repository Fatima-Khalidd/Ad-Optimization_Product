# Data handling agreement

Send this unprompted, before anyone asks for it — with the first email in which
you request a file. For a student asking a business for their advertising data,
volunteering this does more for trust than anything you can say in a call.

Copy the text below into an email or a one-page PDF. Fill in the two bracketed
fields. It is written to be read in a minute by a non-lawyer.

---

## Data handling — Ad Spend Optimization

**Between:** Fatima Khalid, trading as Ad Spend Optimization ("we")
**And:** [CLIENT BUSINESS NAME] ("you")
**Date:** [DATE]

### What you send us

An export of your advertising performance data: spend, impressions, clicks and
conversions, broken down by placement, age group and time of day.

That is all we need. We do **not** need, and are not asking for:

- access to your advertising account, Business Manager or any password
- your customers' names, phone numbers, emails or order details
- your website, CRM or payment systems

If your export contains campaign names you would rather we did not see, replace
them before sending. The analysis does not use them.

### What we do with it

- Run the analysis, produce your report, and discuss it with you.
- Nothing else. We do not share it with anyone, we do not sell it, we do not
  show it to other clients, and we do not use it to train any software.

### Who can see it

Only us. Where the file is stored on hosted services, those are Vercel, Railway
and Supabase (Mumbai region), which hold it on our instructions and do not use
it. If you send the file by email or WhatsApp, it also sits in that inbox and on
our computer until deleted.

### How long we keep it

- **Free audit:** we delete the file and the report once the audit is delivered
  and you have had a chance to discuss it — or immediately, if you ask.
- **Ongoing client:** uploads and reports are kept for 24 months so that
  month-on-month comparisons are possible. Invoices and payment records are kept
  for 6 years, as tax rules require. Those contain amounts and dates, not your
  advertising data.

### Deleting it

Email us and we delete it. We will confirm in writing within 7 days. You do not
need to give a reason, and there is no charge.

### What we promise about the analysis

- Every figure comes from the rows you sent. You can check the arithmetic
  yourself, and we will show our working on request.
- We will not overstate waste. The same wasted rupee appears in the placement,
  age and time-of-day views; we report the largest single view rather than
  adding the three together.
- Segments too small to be statistically meaningful are shown to you but never
  flagged as waste.
- If we find little or no waste in your account, we will tell you that plainly.

### What this is not

The report is an estimate based on your data. It is not a guarantee of savings,
and it is not financial advice. What you change in your account is your
decision.

## Notes for you (do not send this part)

- For a free audit, a signature is usually unnecessary — sending this text in an
  email is enough, and asking for a signature can make a small business wary.
  Keep the signature block for paying clients.
- If a client asks for an NDA of their own, sign theirs. Do not negotiate; at
  this stage the relationship is worth more than the clause.
- Do the deletion when you say you will. One client discovering you still have
  their file a year later would undo everything the rest of this is for.
